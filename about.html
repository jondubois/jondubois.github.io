About me

I'm a software developer and entrepreneur with 15+ years of experience building developer tools, real-time infrastructure, and no-code platforms.

My early projects include Jumbo Website Manager (2011), an intuitive drag-and-drop CMS, and JCombo, a full-stack JavaScript framework predating Meteor. From JCombo, I extracted SocketCluster — a real-time WebSocket framework still widely used in production today.

In 2016, SocketCluster was adopted by Lisk, a top-10 blockchain platform that reached a $4B market cap, where I contributed as a core infrastructure developer and delegate validator. I subsequently built Capitalisk, a quantum-resistant cryptocurrency with a decentralized exchange.

My newest project, Saasufy, is a no-code backend platform designed to solve the real challenges of production software — multi-tenancy, authentication, real-time updates, and security at scale — with Insnare, a people discovery platform, built entirely on it as a proof of concept.

I'm currently working as a developer at an AI company, bringing deep experience in real-time systems, blockchain infrastructure, and full-stack architecture to applied AI development.
